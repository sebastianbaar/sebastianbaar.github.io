Information about collected and processed data

This app does not collect any data when you use it. All processing is done locally on your device, so no internet connection is needed.

App analytics & privacy

Apple provides an app analytics function for developers. The data is provided to the developer by Apple in anonymized form. This function is independent of the app and can be switched off here: Settings → Privacy → Analytics → Share With App Developers.

Storage of user settings

By default, user settings are stored locally on your device. The following data is stored when you save or edit an album: album name, selected images and selected interval. When you delete the app, these data are deleted as well.
